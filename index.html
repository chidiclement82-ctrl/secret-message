    How is this private?
    • Your complaint is locked on your device before it's sent, using my public key (ECDH P-256 and AES-256-GCM). Only my private key can unlock it.
    • The database only ever stores the locked version. Neither the database company nor anyone who breaks into it can read your complaint.
    • My replies are encrypted with a key only your device and I have. Your private link carries it after the #, which is never sent to any server.
    • My private key never leaves my devices unlocked. It's stored locked with my passphrase, which is never sent anywhere.
    • There are no accounts, no tracking and no cookies, and I don't ask who you are. I can't tell who sent a complaint.
    • Messages are padded so their length doesn't give away what's in them.
