How is this private?
  • Your message is locked on your device before it's sent, using my public key (ECDH P-256 and AES-256-GCM). Only my private key can unlock it.
  • The database only ever stores the locked version. Neither the database company nor anyone who breaks into it can read your message.
  • My private key never leaves my devices unlocked. It's stored locked with my passphrase, which is never sent anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no tracking and no cookies. Unless you add your name, I can't tell who sent a message.
  • Messages are padded so their length doesn't give away what's in them.
